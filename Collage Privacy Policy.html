Collage — Privacy Policy

Your quotes stay on your phone.

Effective date: September 27, 2026

Collage is built to work offline, on your own device. This policy explains, in plain language, exactly what the app does and does not do with your information — because the honest answer is: almost nothing leaves your phone.

01What we collect

Collage does not collect any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, and no name, email address, phone number, or location ever requested or transmitted.

No account required No analytics No advertising No trackers

Everything you create in the app — favorites, personal notes on a quote, words you save, quotes you write yourself — is stored in a local database directly on your device, using standard iOS storage. We have no access to it and no copy of it exists anywhere else.

02Content syncing

Collage downloads its library of quotes, authors, and categories from our backend so the app's content can be updated over time. This is a one-way content download: your device asks for quotes, and quotes are sent back. No personal identifier, account, or usage data is attached to that request, and none of your own content (favorites, notes, custom quotes) is ever uploaded to it.

Once content has synced, Collage works fully offline.

03Notifications

If you turn on the daily quote reminder, it is scheduled entirely on your device using iOS's local notification system. No server is involved, no push token is generated, and nothing about your reminder schedule is sent anywhere.

04Backup & export

If you export your collection to JSON or Excel, that file is created and saved through iOS's own share sheet, under your control. We never receive a copy of it. Importing quotes works the same way, entirely on your device.

05Third parties

Collage contains no third-party advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, and no crash-reporting SDKs. The only network connection the app makes is the one-way content sync described above.

06Children's privacy

Collage does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information from any user. The app is suitable for a general audience.

07Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future feature introduces an optional account or a paid subscription — this page will be updated and the effective date above will change accordingly.
