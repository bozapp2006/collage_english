Collage English — Privacy Policy

Your quotes stay on your phone.

Effective date: September 27, 2026

In short: Collage English is designed with privacy in mind. Personal content you create in the app stays on your device. We do not require an account and we do not sell, rent, or use your personal information for advertising or tracking.


1. Information We Collect

Collage does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, location, or other personal information to use the app.

The app does not intentionally collect personal information for advertising, profiling, or tracking purposes.

2. Content Stored on Your Device

The following content may be stored locally on your iPhone or iPad:

  • Favorite quotes
  • Your personal notes attached to quotes
  • Saved words
  • Quotes you write yourself
  • Other app preferences and locally created content


This content is intended to remain on your device and is not transmitted to us as part of the normal operation of the app.

3. Quote Library and App Content

Collage may connect to our content service to retrieve and update the quote library, authors, categories, and other non-personal application content.

These updates are used to keep the quote collection current. They are not used to create a personal profile about you.

4. Analytics and Tracking

Collage does not use third-party advertising trackers or cross-app tracking technologies.

We do not use your personal information to build advertising profiles or sell information to advertisers.

5. Advertising

Collage does not sell your personal information to advertisers. The app is not designed to use your personal content for targeted advertising.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Because Collage does not require an account and your personal notes, favorites, saved words, and user-written quotes are stored locally, this personal content is not routinely shared with us.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the services used to provide and update Collage content. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Collage does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app is designed to minimize the collection of personal data.

9. Your Choices

You can remove the app and its locally stored data from your device using the standard iOS controls. Content created and stored locally by the app is under your control through the device on which it is stored.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the app or applicable requirements. The updated version will be published at this location with a revised effective date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of Collage, please contact the app developer through the support contact provided on the app's App Store listing.
